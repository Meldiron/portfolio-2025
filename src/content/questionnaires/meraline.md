These are the questions an agent asked me before writing this case study, and my answers exactly as I typed them, typos included. I answered them in a form, and the agent turned them into the article.

## What the agent found

Before asking anything, the agent read the code, the commit history and the live app. This is the summary it showed me.

A native macOS 26 menu bar app. ⌥ Space opens a floating Liquid Glass panel over any app without stealing focus; answers stream in, and chats live only in memory, deleted 30 minutes after the last message. No backend, no analytics, API keys in the Keychain.

It grew three modes: LLM (Apple Intelligence on-device by default, Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama/custom), Agent (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode with MCP servers, Allow/Deny permission cards and its own present_files MCP tool), and Decision (System One models like TypeSafe's Jev answering yes/no, choices or levels with confidence, plus Live and Improve). On top: context capture (selection, clipboard, screenshot), ⌘K actions, Show What Changed diffs, tear-off notes, presets, 8 word games, a usage/cost dashboard and a meraline:// URL scheme.

Swift 6 with strict concurrency, SwiftUI + AppKit, ~32k lines of app code and 825 tests. Signed, notarized DMG, Sparkle auto-updates every 15 minutes with a beta channel, Homebrew cask, build attestations — all set up on day one. README screenshots are generated by the test suite with ScreenCaptureKit.

The trailer is a 96-second Remotion film cut to a 116 BPM soundtrack assembled from GarageBand Apple Loops by a script, recorded by driving the real v1.4.0 app with scripted input. Made on the evening of day two. The rendered video isn't in the repo and I couldn't find it published anywhere.

## Origin & vision

### What made you build Meraline?

_A specific moment or annoyance? What were you using before (ChatGPT app, Raycast AI, Spotlight…) and what was missing?_

Raycast made BYOK paid, and I was looking for alternatives for quick chat AI, but couldnt find any good and open source.

### Where does the name "Meraline" come from?

Ephemeral -> Meral -> Meraline (female assistant)

### Why is "let it go" the core idea instead of a privacy toggle?

_30-minute chats, nothing on disk. Do you personally never want history? Has anyone asked for persistence?_

Inspired from quick AI, chats are not meant to be persistant, you should just ask, get answer, and its gone. It remains for a bit in case you closed it by mistake, or need to recall something you just talked about.

### Who is it for?

_Yourself, developers, privacy-minded people, Appwrite colleagues…?_

Anyone, but mainly me and my friends.

## Building it

### How did you build it with Claude?

_248/249 commits co-authored by Claude. How did you split work between models, what did "several sessions edit this checkout at once" look like, and did the 96 KB AGENTS.md make a real difference?_

Most of work with Opus, with some critical flows by Fable.

### v1.0 shipped an hour after a 2,700-line first commit — was there a prototype before the repo?

No prototype, directly to 1.0, why not. product is best tested when used.

### Why native Swift for macOS 26 only, and how was working with Liquid Glass?

Precisely for liquid glass, and best compatibility with macOS. no plan for other platforms right now.

### What was hardest?

**Streaming performance / rendering**, **Release pipeline (signing, notarization, Sparkle)**

This project was my first experiene with Apple developer program, learning a lot of quirks of all the keys, signatures, certificates, ...

### Which providers do you actually use day to day, and why support so many?

I use OpenRouter for LLM, because I like ability to switch between any models, for a little extra price. Plus I prefer pay per usage for LLM, because I use it very very little..

For agent, I use Claude max, because I have company one that I can use. I would still use Claude probably, just cheeaper plan.

And for decision mode, I use Jev, as it's early and there arent many options. I will be switching to OpenRouter when I run out of Jev credits.

### Why build a full notarized DMG + Sparkle + beta channel + Homebrew pipeline on day one?

I wanted to learn to do this properly on this project. I also took a lot of inspiration from OpenClip to which I controbuted a few pull requests before startign Meraline project.

### Screenshots are generated by the test suite. Did that pay off? Would you reuse it?

I love it. Automated screenshots are huge win for quality assesment.

## Features & scope

### What's your connection to TypeSafe / Jev and Ollaya, and why add Decision mode?

_Partnership, your own thing, early adopter? Who are Live and Improve for?_

Decision mode was idea due to rising popularity on this topic, and I saw huge potential that no other AI clients did yet - both realtime text decisions, as well as bulk processing.

### Why word games in an AI utility? Does anyone play them?

I play them. I like playing them when on boring meets to stay sharp. For example I like rhyme training, and word crafting, liek scrabble.

### From "tiny app for quick questions" to three modes and 15 releases in 13 days — intentional? What would you cut?

Intentional, and I like it this way. Passion allowed me to go full force for 2 weeks, building a fully finished product that serves me and my friends great value. both me and agent are pretty-much out of ideas what could be improved next, only tiny details.

### Your favourite feature or detail?

Realtime decision mode. I can add question like "Flirt?" and learn my flirting skills, hihi.

## The trailer

### Why make a beat-synced product film the day after launch, and why Remotion instead of a video editor?

Remotion was tool agent waas confident with for ability to make draft, and then keep making edits to improve quality with my experience.

### What was the hardest or most fun part of making it?

_Driving the real app with scripted input, the Apple Loops soundtrack script, keeping the edit in sync after re-recordings…_

I liked running long-running agents runnig similations on my laptop, trying to break the app. it was fun looking at what wild ideas agent had to crash the app or break UI.
